## Authored orca source
**Killer whale — Junskie Pastilan / pasilan**, [original publisher](https://blendswap.com/blend/7791) · [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

Public glTF redistribution: [WebGL Fundamentals, Skinning](https://webglfundamentals.org/webgl/lessons/webgl-skinning.html). The complete obtained five-file source folder is preserved with hashes, pinned mirror commit and original license. The bundled legacy license has an incorrect title; the original creator page, author and legacy65255 source identifier were independently reconciled. Original .blend acquisition is not claimed.

Modifications: source geometry normalization, UV-preserving articulated mandibular split, palate and modeled teeth, production swimming deformation, intentional age/dorsal variants, poses, materials, lighting and composition. These derivatives are not claimed to be the source creator's original jaw rig or scientific individual reconstructions.

## Picture interpretation
All R5 pictures are newly rendered explanatory illustrations/reconstructions, not footage of the narrated incidents. Human figures are a generic licensed cast, not likenesses of named surfers, divers, historical whalers or particular survivors. The chronological incident diagram is schematic rather than a biological size comparison. The liver cutaway is an explanatory two-lobed illustration, not a scan of an individual shark. Natural Earth geography is generalized and is not for navigation. The swimming comparison uses an explicitly displayed quarter-speed clock and an illustrative brief burst interval, not a claim of sustained swimming endurance.
