## Picture and narration
This edit uses a new original animated2D director as its central visual language, a newly authored continuous body/skull3D leopard-seal model, exactly four original real photographs, and four genuine camera-video sources. The rejected earlier director, assembled3D head, Snow human model and earlier reconstructed scenes are not used. The ten approved voice00 recordings play at1.0x on the retained651-second timeline. The original master text remains unchanged on disk. The sole authorized spoken exception is the previously recorded non-graphic section04 sentence; no new TTS is used. Contextual illustrations do not purport to be recordings of Paul Nicklen, Gareth Wood or the2003 attack.
