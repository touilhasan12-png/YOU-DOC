# Something That Plans — footage, artwork and sources

## Picture and narration
Original programmatic colored illustration: dimensioned 3-D animal meshes, articulated crew and vessels, physical interaction reconstructions, and geographic diagrams. No image-generation tools or generated-image assets were used.

The approved 1,730-word script and original narrator master are preserved. The user-authorized ending derivative retains all spoken words and CTA, with pitch/formant-preserving tempo correction. Reconstructions and anatomy/size diagrams are illustrations, not event footage or measured individual telemetry. Calf/juvenile/adult drawings do not assert exact historical body lengths.

## Maps and fonts
- Real land geometry: Natural Earth, public domain. The current regional maps use the actual **110m** land geometry, not a claimed high-resolution coastline or exact incident tracks. https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
- Typography: Barlow Condensed and Source Sans 3. The corresponding font license files are preserved in the production workspace.

## Scientific and historical context
The script's historical dates and reported counts are presented as narrated-period records, not live totals. Illustrative case maps identify real regions without fabricating individual research tracks. Existing production research references and archive receipts remain in `SOURCES.md` and the design/QA folders. Prior film editions and their sharing were not overwritten or changed.
